## 🧠 6. Study Strategy & Roadmap

**🔹 Step 1 – Core Theory (Concepts Repeated Every Year)**
* Data Mining architecture
* KDD process
* OLAP vs OLTP
* ETL and data preprocessing
* Metadata types

📖 **Study approach:** Make short notes and flowcharts — every exam repeats at least one of these.

**🔹 Step 2 – Algorithmic Understanding (High Scoring)**
* K-Means Clustering
* Apriori Algorithm
* Naïve Bayes
* Decision Tree
* DBSCAN

🧮 **Focus:** Learn steps → work at least 2 solved examples per algorithm. Use small data tables to practice manually.

**🔹 Step 3 – Analytical Numericals (Practice Zone)**
* Accuracy, Recall, Precision formula
* Statistical measures (Mean, Median, Variance)
* Dendrogram drawing (Hierarchical Clustering)

✍️ **Practice manually** — each paper includes at least one numerical (10 marks).

**🔹 Step 4 – Integrative & Conceptual Topics**
* Market Basket Analysis (Support, Confidence)
* Multilevel/Multidimensional association rules
* Web Mining types (Structure, Content, Usage)

💡 Usually appear in 10-mark theory or application-style questions.
