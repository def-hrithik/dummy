## 🧠 4. Topic-Wise Priority Ranking (for Study Plan)

| Rank | Topic | Why Important | Study Tips |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 🥇 1 | **OSI & TCP/IP Models** | Fundamental, repeated every year | Make a clear table comparing layers, functions, protocols. |
| 🥈 2 | **Routing Algorithms** (Distance Vector, Dijkstra) | Always appears | Practice examples; memorize formulae and diagrams. |
| 🥉 3 | **TCP Protocols** (3-way handshake, flow control) | Networking essential | Learn states: SYN, SYN-ACK, ACK + diagram. |
| 4 | **IP Addressing** (IPv4 Header, Subnetting) | Common analytical question | Solve multiple subnetting problems. |
| 5 | **Error Detection** (CRC, Hamming Code) | Frequent numerical | Practice bitwise operations manually. |
| 6 | **Sliding Window Protocols** (Go-Back-N, Selective Repeat) | Consistently repeated | Understand difference and sequence diagrams. |
| 7 | **SDN Concepts** (NoX, PoX, OpenFlow) | Modern topic, always appears | Compare architecture and flow of control/data plane. |
| 8 | **DNS, DHCP, NAT** | Always part of short notes | Prepare concise definitions and functions. |
| 9 | **Network Topologies & Devices** | Short question but easy marks | Prepare diagrams and examples. |
| 10 | **Cisco Network Design Models** (PPDIOO, SONA) | Always a design-based theory question | Focus on stages and purposes. |
