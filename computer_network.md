## 📚 Predicted Top 15 Important Exam Questions

**1.** Explain the **need for layered designing** in networking and communication.

**2.** Compare the **OSI reference model with TCP/IP model** and draw a neat diagram.

**3.** Differentiate between **TCP and UDP protocols** [core features].

**4.** Explain the functionality of **Sliding Window Protocol** (including Go-Back-N technique).

**5.** Discuss the different **classes of IPv4 addressing technique** and the concept of **subnetting** (include calculations).

**6.** Describe the **Channel Allocation Problem** and the working of **CSMA/CD protocol**, including computation of minimum frame size.

**7.** What is **ALOHA**? Explain the difference between **Pure ALOHA and Slotted ALOHA**.

**8.** Write short notes on **Parity Check and Hamming Code** (with example encoding).

**9.** Explain **Distance Vector Routing** and **Link State Routing** algorithms with suitable examples.

**10.** Elaborate on **TCP Connection Management** (Three-way handshake, timers, flow control mechanism).

**11.** Explain the architecture of **Software Defined Networking (SDN)**, including building blocks, Open Flow controllers (NOX/POX).

**12.** Compare and contrast **Classful vs Classless Addressing** in IPv4 (with disadvantages of classful).

**13.** Write short notes on **DNS** and its components.

**14.** Discuss **Network Design Methodologies** (Cisco PPDIOO, SONA, Hierarchical models).

**15.** Differentiate **Packet Switched vs Circuit Switched networks**, describing key pros and cons and suitability.
